Part 2 · Unknown Image, 10 pts

The docent's notes

You can't study a piece you've never seen — but you can study the questions and the visual shortcuts that usually give a period away.

Ask, in order

  1. What's it made of? Material narrows the period fast.
  2. What's shown, and how — idealized, naturalistic, symbolic, abstracted?
  3. Any cultural or religious clues in the subject or symbols?
  4. What specific visual evidence connects it to something we studied?
  5. Given all that — what period, and why that one and not another?
